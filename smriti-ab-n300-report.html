n=200 → n=300: the effect cooled and shifted

At n=200 the observation stack looked near-significant (+4.8 pp, p≈0.095). Scaling to n=300 shrank the overall lift and revealed a knowledge-update regression that was a small gain at n=200.

interpretation · honest read

What the n=300 run is really saying

  • The overall story is murky. +1.0 pp is directionally positive, but the McNemar p-value (~0.77) means this difference is easily explained by chance.
  • Multi-session is the clear win. +18.5 pp and a net +10 flips suggest observations help the model bridge evidence across sessions.
  • Knowledge-update regressed hard. From +2.9 pp at n=200 to −13.0 pp at n=300 (net −7 flips). The extra synthesized context may be distracting the model from the latest superseding fact.
  • Temporal-reasoning still gains, but less. Cooled from +17.6 pp to +7.4 pp — real, but not the dramatic effect seen earlier.
  • Recommendation: do not ship the stack as-is. Diagnose why observations hurt knowledge-update; consider enabling them only for multi-session / temporal queries, or guard the digest injection so it cannot override explicit supersession.
